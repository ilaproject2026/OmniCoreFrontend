### Quick Summary:
- **Ecosystem Core Concept**: Vertical-driven access control where the OmniCore Project Admin grants specific industry vertical entitlements (Courier, Bus, Cold Chain, etc.) to customer organizations.
- **Roles Hierarchy**:
  1. `OmniCore Project Admin` (Master Super Admin)
  2. `Customer Admin` (Organization Manager)
  3. `Vertical Operations Staff` (Operational Execution)
  4. `Customer Staff Member` (Task Viewers / Field Crew)
- **14 Specialized Verticals**: Taxi & Cab, Tourist Taxi, Tourist Bus, Corporate Fleet, Corporate Shuttle, Courier Express, Last-Mile, Freight Logistics, Packers & Movers, Cold Chain, Bulk Tanker, Heavy Machinery, Project Logistics, B2B Dedicated Contracts, and Enterprise Multi-Vertical Hub.
- **Subscription Tiers**: Basic Starter ($149), Standard Fleet ($399), Corporate Enterprise ($899), OmniCore Enterprise ($1,899).
- **Featured Add-ons**: 9 specialized modular packs for IoT, AI Routing, Telematics, Maintenance Pro, and APIs.
